Before you ask

A few useful answers.

Can I book a service through this page?

No. Juniper Home Care is fictional. This page demonstrates website design and does not accept bookings, enquiries, or payments.

What would a real website need before launch?

The owner's approved business name, service list, service area, contact details, hours, and any properly supported qualifications. Hosting and publication would require separate approval.

Does this page collect my information?

No. There are no forms, analytics, cookies, external fonts, or third-party scripts. All artwork and styling are included in this page.

Could this layout work for another local business?

Yes. The introduction, three-service menu, service-area section, FAQs, and contact area can be adapted to an approved business brief.
